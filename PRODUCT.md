# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Hiring managers and recruiters evaluating Thathsara for software engineering and DevOps roles.
- Engineers, researchers, and other potential collaborators looking for credible technical partners.
- Prospective freelance clients assessing whether Thathsara can own and deliver production web and cloud work end to end.

Visitors should be able to understand Thathsara's capabilities, inspect representative work and experience, review the résumé, and make contact.

## Product Purpose

thathsara.dev is Thathsara Madusha's personal portfolio. It establishes professional credibility through concrete work, experience, research, and technical craft, then gives interested employers, collaborators, and clients a direct path to continue the conversation.

Success means the right visitor quickly understands what Thathsara can do, finds credible evidence for it, and follows through by viewing the résumé, exploring linked work, or getting in touch.

## Positioning

Thathsara works end to end across software systems, cloud infrastructure, reliability, and product-facing implementation. The portfolio substantiates that range with shipped work and technically ambitious artifacts rather than presenting a generic list of technologies.

## Operating Context

The product is a public, single-page portfolio at `thathsara.dev`. Visitors move through an introduction, professional focus, selected work, current product work, a technical explanation of the live 3D figure, career and education history, and contact options. The site links outward to source code, professional profiles, supporting technologies, and a downloadable résumé.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site is built with Astro and Svelte, with Three.js powering the live point-cloud figure.
- It is statically deployable and must remain usable as a public web portfolio.
- The interactive 3D portrait is a real browser-rendered artifact and a meaningful demonstration of technical craft.
- Current product truth includes software engineering and DevOps work across Go, Rust, TypeScript, React, Kubernetes, Terraform, AWS, observability, and CI/CD.
- Current career evidence includes a completed WSO2 software engineering internship, ongoing undergraduate research at the University of Colombo, a BSc (Hons) Computer Science program at UCSC, and prior freelance web development.
- Current project evidence includes Seer, Home Lab, and CareerSync.
- Claims must remain grounded in repository content or user-supplied evidence. Do not invent clients, employers, project outcomes, testimonials, benchmarks, or availability details.
- The repository currently states that Thathsara is available for work. Treat this as changeable product data that should be reconfirmed when materially updating calls to action.

## Brand Commitments

- Product and domain name: `thathsara.dev`.
- Public identity: Thathsara Madusha, software engineer and DevOps practitioner.
- Voice: direct, technically precise, confident without inflated claims, and willing to explain how the work was made.
- Preserve the first-person authorship of the portfolio and its emphasis on craft, reliability, clarity, and maintainable engineering.

## Product Principles

1. Prove capability through specific work, outcomes, and inspectable artifacts.
2. Make technical breadth coherent by connecting software, systems, cloud, and reliability into one end-to-end practice.
3. Help every primary audience reach the evidence and next action relevant to them without ambiguity.
4. Keep claims honest, current, and traceable to real experience or project material.
5. Let technical ambition support comprehension and credibility rather than obscure the portfolio's content.
